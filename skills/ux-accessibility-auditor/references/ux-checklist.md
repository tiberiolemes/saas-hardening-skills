# UX checklist for critical journeys

For each journey, record the expected and observed states:

- entry point, prerequisites, and navigation;
- loading, disabled, progress, success, empty, error, retry, and permission-denied states;
- validation timing, field preservation, focus after errors, and clear next action;
- prevention of double submission and accidental destructive actions;
- save, edit, delete, export, upload, payment, and session-expiry feedback;
- responsive behavior at narrow and wide viewports;
- network delay, refresh, back navigation, interrupted request, and partial failure;
- consistent terminology, hierarchy, visual feedback, and product identity.

Prefer a small correction that helps users complete the task and recover from failure. Verify that the change does not hide security boundaries or alter business rules.
